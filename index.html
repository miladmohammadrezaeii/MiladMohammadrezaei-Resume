Lithium-Ion Battery Research

Health Estimation from Partial Operating Data: Developed physical health-feature extraction and selection methods for partial charge/discharge profiles, addressing battery operation without complete cycles. Evaluated the approach using the NASA Random Walk datasets with irregular operating profiles.

Transferable Online Health Estimation: Developed a framework that learns from other batteries to estimate the health of a battery without its own ageing dataset. Identified shared physical characteristics and normalized battery-specific differences to improve model transferability across cells and capacities. Evaluated the framework on NASA Random Walk, Oxford Battery, and CALCE CS2 and CX2 datasets; published in IEEE Transactions on Transportation Electrification (2024).

SoC–OCV Analysis and Parameter Estimation: Investigated changes in the state-of-charge–open-circuit-voltage (SoC–OCV) curve over battery lifetime. Developed an optimization algorithm for simultaneous estimation of internal parameters and state of health during ageing, and evaluated it using partial NASA battery data.

Dynamic and Relaxation Features: Investigated battery-health and lifetime estimation using relaxation and dynamic-response features. Collaborated with Dr. Farshid Naseri on fusion of ageing features from dynamic and rest conditions, resulting in an accepted IECON 2026 paper.

Chemistry-Related Features: Completed a simulation study using chemistry-related parameters independent of SoC in an ML algorithm for battery lifetime and parameter estimation. Evaluated the method on NASA Random Walk data to reduce dependence on whether operating records contain partial or complete charge/discharge profiles.

Industrial Battery Monitoring: Developed MATLAB-based software for monitoring and analysing lead-acid and lithium-ion batteries, and battery-parameter estimation algorithms for an industrial company in Iran. Experience also includes battery-system design and thermal analysis for energy-storage applications.
